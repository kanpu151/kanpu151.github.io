Reel Autopost

Reel Autopost is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

The app accesses only the owner's own Google account, and only to upload videos (youtube.upload scope). It does not collect, store, sell or share any data of other users. Credentials are kept in private storage owned by the app owner.
